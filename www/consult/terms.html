Dietician chat: terms & refunds

Last updated: November 2026

What you get

A private, one-to-one text chat with a qualified dietician who works with dietO. The price and length of a chat are shown before you pay. Your chat time starts only when the dietician joins, not when you pay.

Nutrition guidance, not medical treatment

Dieticians give general nutrition and diet guidance. They do not diagnose illnesses, prescribe medicines or replace your doctor. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or take medication, follow your doctor’s advice. In an emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital.

Your details

With your consent, the dietician sees your name, phone number, the question you write, and your dietO diet-test answers (such as age, weight, goal, health conditions and allergies). We use this only to run your chat, keep a record of it, and handle payments and support. We don’t sell your data. You can ask us to delete your chat history by writing to us at the email below.

Extending a chat

When your time is nearly up, you can add more minutes for the price shown. Extra time is added immediately after the payment succeeds.

Refunds

  • Live chat: if no dietician joins within the waiting time shown on the screen, you can tap “Get a full refund”.
  • Booked chat: if your dietician hasn’t started the chat within the time shown after your booking, you can tap “Get a full refund”.
  • Paid but nothing happened: if money left your account but the chat didn’t start, write to us and we’ll refund you in full.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually arrive in 5–7 working days.
  • Once a dietician has joined, the chat time is used, so unused minutes are not refunded if either side ends the chat early. If you have a genuine problem with a chat, write to us and we’ll look into it.

Respectful use

Please keep chats respectful. Dieticians can end a chat that is abusive or unrelated to diet and nutrition; in that case we may refund or not, at our discretion.

Payments

Payments are processed securely by Razorpay. dietO never sees or stores your card or UPI details.

Contact

Questions, refunds or complaints: health@dieto.health
