Refunds & Cancellations

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains when you can cancel and get your money back for dietO services, sold by Blackspace Applications Pvt. Ltd.. dietO sells digital services only; there are no physical goods or shipping.

If you paid and didn’t get the service, you get a full refund. Most refunds happen automatically or with one tap in the app.

1. Live chat

  • If no dietician joins within 3 minutes, a “Get a full refund” button appears in the chat.
  • If no dietician joins within 5 minutes, we refund you automatically.
  • You can cancel while waiting, before a dietician joins, for a full refund.
  • Once a dietician joins, the minutes are being used, so unused minutes aren’t refunded if the chat is ended early. If there was a genuine problem (the dietician left, or the chat didn’t work), write to us and we’ll make it right.

2. Booked chat

  • If the dietician hasn’t started the chat within 10 minutes of the booked time, you can get a full refund in the app.
  • To cancel a booking, write to us at least 2 hours before the booked time for a full refund.

3. Personalised diet plan

  • The plan is normally sent within 24 hours. If it hasn’t arrived within 48 hours of payment, you can get a full refund in the app.
  • Once the plan has been sent, it isn’t refundable, but you can message your dietician about changes for 14 days.

4. Minute packs

  • Pack minutes stay in your dietO wallet and don’t expire while your account is active.
  • If you haven’t used any minutes from a pack, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of buying it.
  • Once minutes have been used, the rest of that pack isn’t refundable.
  • If a chat paid with wallet minutes is refunded, the minutes go back to your wallet.

5. dietO Care

  • You can cancel within 48 hours of buying for a full refund, if you haven’t used any Care chat minutes and your plan hasn’t been sent.
  • After that, Care isn’t refundable, but it continues until the end of its 30 days.

6. Free chats

Free chats involve no payment, so there’s nothing to refund.

7. Paid but something went wrong

If money left your account but the payment failed, or a chat didn’t start, the bank usually returns it automatically within 5–7 working days. If it doesn’t, email health@dieto.health with your registered mobile number and the date, and we’ll refund you in full.

8. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card, net banking or wallet) through Cashfree Payments, usually within 5–7 working days of approval. Payments made with dietO wallet minutes are refunded as minutes. You’ll see each refund in the app under Account → Payments.

9. Contact

For refunds or cancellations: health@dieto.health. We reply within 2 working days.
