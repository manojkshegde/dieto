This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and the choices you have. It is written to meet India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

In short: we collect what we need to build your diet plan and run your chats. Your health details are shown only to the dietician you chat with, with your consent. We don’t sell your data or show ads. You can download or delete your data from the app at any time.

2. Health information and your consent

Health details are sensitive. Before a chat starts we ask you to agree to share your diet-test answers with the dietician. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app (Account → Privacy); after that, new chats won’t include those details. Dieticians see your data only for chats you start with them, and are bound by confidentiality.

3. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers who help us run dietO, and only what they need:

  • Dieticians working with dietO: for chats and plans you request.
  • Cashfree Payments India Pvt. Ltd.: to process payments and refunds.
  • MSG91: to send sign-in codes by WhatsApp or SMS.
  • Brevo: to send emails about your plan (you can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time).
  • Google Firebase: to deliver push notifications.
  • Our hosting provider: our servers, where your data is stored.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it, for example to a court or government authority. We never sell personal data or use it for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

  • Photos and PDFs shared in chats: deleted automatically 90 days after they are shared.
  • Account, plan, chats and progress: for as long as you keep your account, then deleted when you delete it.
  • Payment records and invoices: kept for 8 years, as Indian tax law requires, even after you delete your account.
  • Technical logs: usually up to 90 days.

5. Your rights and choices

  • Access and download: in the app, go to Account → Privacy → Download my data.
  • Correction: edit your details in the app, or write to us.
  • Deletion: in the app, go to Account → Privacy → Delete my account. This removes your account, chats, files, plans and progress (except invoices we must keep). You can also email health@dieto.health from your registered number’s account details and we’ll do it for you.
  • Notifications: turn each kind on or off in the app.
  • Withdraw consent and nominate someone to act for you, as the DPDP Act allows.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Shared files are stored outside public web folders and opened only through short-lived signed links. Sign-in uses one-time codes, and access is rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures and will tell you and the authorities about a breach as the law requires.
