Delete your dietO account

Last updated: 18 November 2026

You can delete your dietO account and its data at any time. dietO is a brand of Blackspace Applications Pvt. Ltd..

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the dietO app or dieto.health/consult and sign in with your mobile number.
  2. Go to Account → Privacy → Delete my account.
  3. Confirm with the one-time code sent to your phone. Your account is deleted straight away.

By email

Write to health@dieto.health from any address with the subject “Delete my dietO account” and your registered mobile number. We’ll confirm it’s you by a one-time code or a call, and delete the account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, phone, email
  • Diet-test answers, meal plans, meal and water logs, weight entries
  • Chat messages, and photos and PDFs you shared
  • Notifications, device tokens and preferences

What we keep, and for how long

Payment records and GST invoices are kept for 8 years, as Indian tax law requires. They’re kept only for accounting and legal purposes. Server backups are overwritten within 30 days.
